Controls

  • Drag the aircraft – move it
  • Drag the circle ahead of the nose – turn it
  • Mouse wheel over the aircraft – heading ±5°, over the map – zoom
  • Double-click on the map – move the aircraft there
  • ← / → – heading ±1° (with Shift ±10°)
  • Space – fly / stop
  • OBS knob – drag, or scroll over the CDI (with Shift ±10°)
  • HSI knobs – left: course (shared with the CDI's OBS), right: heading bug; click the right knob to set the bug to the current heading
